# IEEE ClimateChain Global Hackathon 2026

Source: official rules and event page, retrieved 2026-09-28 via Devpost.
https://ieee-climatechain-hack.devpost.com/

Organized by: IEEE Turkiye Section.

## Evaluation Criteria

Official Devpost criteria:
- **Technical Implementation**: Effective use of Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning, or IoT technologies.
- **Innovation**: Innovative approach to climate and sustainability problems.
- **Impact**: Concrete impact on carbon markets, renewable energy, supply chain, or environmental monitoring.
- **Feasibility**: Practical feasibility and scalability of the solution.

## Timeline and Eligibility

- Date: October 5–25, 2026 (online).
- Participation: Individual or team (students, developers, data scientists).
- Prizes ($3,000 cash pool):
  - 1st Place: $1,500
  - 2nd Place: $1,000
  - 3rd Place: $500

## This Repo

- A small climate claim verifier is available in `grasshopper/realweb/climate.py`.
- Reads public web page climate commitments, verifies them, and writes an audit trail to mock/devnet ledger.
